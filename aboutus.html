About Us

Grade 10–12 student mentors

Our mission is to give middle school writers a welcoming audience and the confidence to keep turning ideas into stories.

PavithCo-founder

Our story

Page Turners was created by high school students who believe storytelling is a skill worth sharing. We started the contest to help middle school writers try, revise, and celebrate their work in a supportive setting.

We wanted younger students to meet thoughtful mentors who understand how a story grows—and to know that their ideas deserve to be read.

Why we started Page Turners

Make writing feel possible

A clear prompt and a supportive process can help a first idea become a complete story.

Build a peer community

Older students can show younger writers that creativity is a craft anyone can grow.
